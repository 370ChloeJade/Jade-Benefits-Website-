ACA Subsidy Calculator for 2027

By Jade Benefits | Fort Lauderdale, FL | Updated October 2026

Open enrollment for 2027 coverage starts November 1. Enter your household size and estimated income to see roughly how much help you could get with premiums, and how close you are to the 400% poverty level cutoff where subsidies stop.

How the calculator works

Your premium tax credit is the price of the benchmark plan minus what you are expected to contribute. The IRS sets your expected contribution as a percentage of income, and for 2027 it ranges from 2.15% at the lowest incomes up to 10.22% for households between 300% and 400% of the federal poverty level. The calculator applies that table, using the 2026 federal poverty guidelines that apply to 2027 coverage.

Why 400% matters now

The enhanced subsidies that removed the income cap have expired, so the 400% line is back. Go even a dollar over it and the credit disappears entirely. If you are self-employed or run a small business, your income may be easier to plan around than you think. Frequently asked questions

How is my ACA premium tax credit calculated?

The credit equals the cost of the benchmark plan (the second-lowest-cost Silver plan in your area) minus your expected contribution. Your expected contribution is a percentage of your household income, set by the IRS each year, and it rises as your income rises.

What income do I enter in the calculator?

Use your estimated modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) for 2027 for everyone in your tax household. For self-employed people that is net profit after business expenses, not gross revenue.

What is the 400% FPL subsidy cliff?

Since the enhanced subsidies expired, households with income above 400% of the federal poverty level no longer qualify for any premium tax credit. A dollar over the line can mean paying full price for the plan.

What if my income is below 100% of the poverty level?

In most cases you are not eligible for marketplace premium tax credits. Florida has not expanded Medicaid, so options can be limited. Contact us and we will review what is available to you.

Is this calculator exact?

No. It is an estimate based on the 2027 IRS applicable percentage table and the 2026 federal poverty guidelines for the 48 contiguous states and DC. Your actual credit is determined when you apply, and reconciled on your tax return.
