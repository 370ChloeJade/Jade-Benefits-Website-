## Posting tips

- LinkedIn: post during business hours (Tue-Thu, 9am-1pm tends to perform best for B2B content)
- Facebook: evenings and weekends tend to get more engagement from a general audience
- Always include the link, and where possible a photo (your branded graphics or a simple photo of you)
- Reply to every comment, even a short "thanks so much!", engagement signals to both platforms that your content is worth showing to more people
