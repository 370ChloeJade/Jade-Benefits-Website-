8. **Greater Fort Lauderdale Chamber of Commerce** — ftlchamber.com — paid membership, but a real local trust signal and backlink
9. **NAIFA (National Association of Insurance and Financial Advisors)** — naifa.org — paid membership, industry credibility plus a directory listing and backlink

## Order of priority

Start with 1–6 first (all free, quick to do). Chamber of Commerce and NAIFA are worth it if you have budget for the membership fee, since both give you a legitimate backlink plus a trust signal that carries real weight for a licensed broker.
