Why a local broker matters in Palm Beach County

Plans, prices, and provider networks can vary meaningfully from one Florida county to the next, and from Boca Raton to Jupiter to Wellington. A broker who works the South Florida market knows which carriers are competitive here and can check your doctors and hospitals against each plan before you enroll. We are easy to reach, and we stay with you through renewals and changes.

Who we help in Palm Beach County

  • Small businesses with 2 to 99 employees, including professional offices, contractors and trades, hospitality, real estate, and medical or dental practices. See our complete small business guide.

  • Early retirees and people under 65 who need coverage before Medicare. Marketplace plans can bridge the gap, and subsidies depend on your income, so planning your income matters.

Check your doctors before you choose

Palm Beach County is served by hospitals and health systems such as Boca Raton Regional Hospital, HCA Florida JFK Hospital, and St. Mary's Medical Center, plus many independent practices. Whether a particular doctor or hospital is in network depends on the plan itself, not only the carrier, so we confirm your providers against each plan we recommend.

Open enrollment for 2027

Open enrollment for 2027 coverage runs November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027, and December 15 is the deadline for coverage that starts January 1. See our open enrollment guide for the full calendar, and use the free subsidy calculator to estimate your help with premiums. If you are close to the 400% income limit, small planning steps can matter, so run the numbers before you pick a plan.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to use a health insurance broker in Palm Beach County?

No. Jade Benefits is paid by the insurance carrier when you enroll, and your premium is the same whether you use a broker or enroll on your own. Our comparisons and ongoing support are free to you.

I am retiring before 65. What are my health insurance options?

Most people in this situation compare ACA marketplace plans, COBRA if available, or a spouse's employer plan. Marketplace subsidies are based on your projected income, so how and when you draw income can change what you pay. We can walk you through the options.

Can my Palm Beach County business get a group health plan?

Generally yes, if you have at least one W-2 employee besides the owner and spouse. Carriers also have participation and contribution requirements, which we explain before you apply.

When is open enrollment for 2027 coverage?

November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027 on HealthCare.gov. Enroll by December 15 for coverage that starts January 1.

Do you serve all of Palm Beach County?

Yes. We work with clients throughout the county by phone, email, and video, and we can meet in person around South Florida when it helps.
